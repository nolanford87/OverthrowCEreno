# Hamlets: round 2 critique

Tested in the game (all 18, both tiers): `round2/measurements.md` and the screenshots in `round2/`.

## Verdict
Measurements clean again: nothing clips, floats or is blind, every class exists. Tier 2 has more in it (blast wall,
bunker or roadside check, wire), but **from the street it still reads as a small change** (look at
`Agia_Triada_T1_top.jpg` vs `_T2_top.jpg`: one bunker appears; Kalithea: a few bags along the street). The step to
tier 2 has to be obvious to a player driving past.

## What would make it obvious
- **Height**: everything is knee to chest high. Add one tall element at the way in: stack the blast wall 2-high
  (Land_HBarrier_Big_F, or two H-barriers with the second dropped on top), or put the approach post on a
  Land_BagBunker_Tower_F with the man on its platform (a guard may stand on a placed tower anywhere).
- **The roadside check must look like a checkpoint**: a bar gate across the road (or concrete barriers in a
  chicane), the bunker or a sandbag position beside it, the occupier's flag there.
- Keep it to about 7 guards; this is mass and visibility, not manpower.

This should be the last pass for the hamlets before the user reviews them in the editor.

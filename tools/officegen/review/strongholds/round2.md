# Strongholds: round 2 critique

Tested in the game (all four, tiers 1-5): `round2/measurements.md` and the screenshots in `round2/` (the tower's
top views are now from 40 m above its roof).

## Verdict
The weapons work now: Pyrgos's statics are fixed and almost every gun has a field. Remaining problems are the tower
men and the overall impression.

## Measured
- **Men on the towers can't see (0 m)**: Kavala's three autoriflemen, Athira's rifleman and autorifleman, Zaros's
  marksman and AT man: the 2.75 m platform guess puts them inside the tower. The next check logs the tower's real
  standing positions (`measurements.md`, "Where a man stands on a placed object", model coordinates); use those.
- **Statics**: Athira HMG 11 m (both tiers), Athira mortar 11.6 m (a mortar can live with it); AT guns "floating"
  0.5-0.7 m (Kavala, Athira) may be a measurement artifact of the AT tripod, to be confirmed by eye.
- The lobby gendarme at 2.8 m is fine: he faces the main door, which is 2.8 m away.
- Pyrgos autorifleman 1.1 m, Athira and Zaros riflemen 1.0 m: turn them out.

## It still doesn't read as a stronghold
In the pictures, Kavala tier 5 is a tall building with a tower, some blocks and hedgehogs; Athira tier 5 a tower and
a few pieces. Tier 4 is a FORT and tier 5 an ABSOLUTE STRONGHOLD in the user's ladder: from the street a player
should see a continuous high perimeter, towers at its corners, heavy weapons, checkpoints with barriers across the
roads. Right now most of the added strength is inside or on the roof. Push the outer layer much harder at tiers 4
and 5: a full 2-high ring (the square in front of Kavala's tower is the obvious place), two or more towers, visible
gun pits, and each approach road blocked by a real checkpoint (H-barrier blocks across the road with a bunker and a
chicane).
